# Faded Pearl

Somewhere deep beneath the surface, you may hear someone crying.

If you follow the sound, you can find a wounded Enderman hiding in the dark. He is not a boss
and he is not another creature waiting to be tamed with one click. He is a Fade — one of the
rare Endermen who escaped the Ender Dragon's influence — and he has every reason to be afraid
of you.

You can choose to help him.

Healing the Fade is only the beginning. Trust grows slowly through the way you treat him, the
places you visit together and the small moments he remembers. At first he may keep his distance.
Later he can follow you, protect you, carry you away from danger, examine things that catch his
attention and react to the world around him. He may notice a flower in your hand, become curious
about an animal, remember a name or comment on something you discover together.

The goal of Faded Pearl is not to give you a stronger wolf. It is to make one unusual companion
feel like somebody who is gradually learning to live beside you.

## Your story with the Fade

- Find a wounded Fade naturally in an underground cave and discover how to heal him.
- Earn trust instead of receiving every ability immediately.
- Watch his appearance and behavior change as your bond develops.
- Give him simple commands: Follow, Stay, Rest and Home.
- Let him protect you, rescue you from danger or carry you during joint teleports.
- Show him interesting items and watch him examine them before returning them.
- See him react to animals, villages, weather, discoveries, names and other Fade companions.
- Unlock the Enderman Journal, whose pages fill with memories, observations, controls and
  recipes as you discover them in play.
- Build a Resonating Anchor to give him a home and call him back to you.
- Craft the permanent Escape Pearl upgrade so an accidental hit from his own player cannot
  hurt him or damage the bond.

The mod includes English and Polish language support. Cave encounters, trust gains and losses,
the Anchor's reward and the recovery delay can be adjusted separately for each world.

## A note about the beta

`1.8.0-beta.3` has completed its singleplayer tests on both a fresh world and a migrated test
world. Multiplayer is available, and an earlier three-player session confirmed that each player
can form a separate bond, but complete dedicated-server testing of restarts, disconnects,
recovery and dimension travel is still unfinished.

For multiplayer, please treat this as an experimental beta: back up the world first and install
the exact same Faded Pearl JAR on the server and on every player's client.

## Requirements

The exact setup used during release testing was:

- Minecraft 1.20.1
- Forge 47.4.22
- Java 17
- GeckoLib 4.8.4
- SmartBrainLib 1.15

Other versions inside the ranges accepted by the mod may work, but they were not the release
test environment.

## Updating an existing world

Close the game, back up the entire world and remove every older Faded Pearl JAR from the active
`mods` folder before installing the new version. Open a copy of the world first and make sure
your Fade, trust, Journal and home are still correct.

Do not downgrade a world that has already been saved by a newer version. To roll back safely,
restore both the older world copy and the matching older set of mods.

Before installing or updating, please read the full
[installation instructions](https://github.com/KrzakZCzarnobyla/Faded-Pearl-Public/blob/main/docs/INSTALLATION.md).
The repository also contains a reference for
[world configuration](https://github.com/KrzakZCzarnobyla/Faded-Pearl-Public/blob/main/docs/CONFIGURATION.md)
and an honest list of the
[current beta limitations](https://github.com/KrzakZCzarnobyla/Faded-Pearl-Public/blob/main/docs/KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md).
