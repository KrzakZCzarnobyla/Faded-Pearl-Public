# Faded Pearl 1.9.0-beta.1

This community beta makes the Fade feel more present during ordinary play. It adds quiet routines,
new things for him to notice, and memories that persist as your shared story grows.

## Highlights

- calm companion routines: shared gaze, resting near the bonded player and settling near home;
- autonomous interest in knowledge and crafting blocks, with safe pathing and Journal guidance;
- persistent relationship memories for villages, diamonds, animals, building, armour, reunions and
  completed night watches;
- five story memories connected with the End portal, the dragon, its egg and returning home;
- a redesigned Resonating Anchor with separate inactive and active appearances;
- contextual behaviour priorities that yield to combat, rescue, carrying, recovery, hazards and
  direct commands.

The Enderman Journal remains the in-game guide. Newly discovered features explain their controls
and important limits without revealing hidden entries too early.

## Beta status

The build passed 438 automated tests, resource validation, a clean development-client startup and
copied-world Save/Quit and dimension-travel checks. Natural presentation frequency, several
interruption cases, the complete End-memory flow and controlled companion recovery remain open for
community testing.

Multiplayer remains experimental. An earlier three-player session confirmed the basic ownership
loop, but full dedicated-server regression is deferred to version 2.5. Back up the world and use
the exact same Faded Pearl JAR on the server and every client.

## Requirements

- Minecraft 1.20.1
- Forge 47.4.22
- Java 17
- GeckoLib 4.8.4
- SmartBrainLib 1.15
